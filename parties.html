Birthday parties that move

Our coaches run 90 minutes of foam pit, trampoline, zip line and games in the gym, then the party room is yours. You bring the cake. We'll wear them out.

Request a party date Looking for Open Gym?

How the party runs

  1. 60 min

    In the gym

    Coach-led fun built for the group's ages: warm-up, obstacle course, games and races, foam pit, trampoline, rings, rope swings, tumbling track and the zip line.

  2. 30 min

    In the party room

    Time for food, cake and presents. Decorate with any theme you like. We provide the room, tables and benches.

Good to know

When
Saturdays and Sundays. Dates depend on availability, so call or request a date.
Guests
Up to 12 children included. Up to 8 more can be added.
Ages
The birthday child can be any age. All guests should be able to join the activities.
Parents
Parents are welcome to watch inside the gym.
What to wear
Leotard or shorts and a t-shirt, bare feet. No belts, jewelry, buckles or dresses.
Waivers
Each guest's parent signs a waiver before entering the gym. The host collects them.
Add-ons
Pizza, birthday cake, ice cream and a chocolate fountain are available for an extra charge.
Reserving
A non-refundable deposit holds your date. We'll share package details when you inquire.
