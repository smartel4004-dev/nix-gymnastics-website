A hometown gym with a world-class coach

NIX is a gymnastics and fitness complex for New Ipswich and the surrounding towns. Our mission: the best programs, staff and equipment we can offer, and a place where kids grow to love sport, fitness and life.

Head coach, boys' and girls' programs

Nikolai

In 2021, Coach Nikolai marked 50 years since he first walked into a gym as a coach. He has prepared gymnasts around the world: 20 years in the former USSR and Belarus, plus Poland, Italy and, since 1995, the United States.

He brings a specialist's eye for conditioning, technique and choreography to every level at NIX, and the patience to teach a four-year-old a forward roll as carefully as a national-level routine. He speaks English, Italian, Russian and Polish.

  • USA Gymnastics professional member
  • National Safety Certified
  • Boys' and girls' programs

Rooted in the community

NIX supports and sponsors community events, and hosts them whenever we can. Our families help the gym grow through seasonal fundraisers, like fall mums and holiday wreaths, that fund new equipment and support our coaches.

We hope every family who walks through our doors leaves with a greater love for sports, fitness and life.
