Moms & Tots

Walking to age 3, with a grown-up

Introduce your toddler to gymnastics side by side. With guided play on mats, low beams and soft shapes, our youngest members build a foundation for every sport they'll try next. Moms, dads and caregivers are all welcome.

  • Following simple instructions
  • Building confidence in a new place
  • Exploring balance, climbing and rolling
  • Enjoying exercise together

Preschool Gymnastics

Ages 4–5

Preschool and kindergarten classes turn big energy into real skills: forward rolls, jumps, swings on the bar and walks along the beam. Short stations keep attention high and every child moving.

  • Coordination and body awareness
  • Strength and flexibility
  • Taking turns and listening to a coach
  • First skills on bars, beam, floor and vault

Recreational Gymnastics

Ages 6–12, plus an advanced group

Our safe and fun recreational classes develop strength, flexibility, balance, coordination and self-esteem. Kids train on every event and move through skills at their own pace. Those ready for a bigger challenge can move up to the advanced group.

  • Bars, beam, floor and vault every season
  • Clear skill goals so kids see their progress
  • Advanced group for kids ready for more
  • A natural path toward the competitive team

Tumbling

Ages 7 and up

Tumbling builds acrobatic skills for cheerleading, dance, snowboarding or overall fitness. By learning flexibility, balance and strength first, athletes are better prepared to enjoy and succeed in every sport they take on, safely.

  • Rolls, cartwheels and round-offs
  • Walkovers and handsprings
  • Safe landings and spotted progressions
  • Flexibility and core strength

Ninja

Ages 4 and up

Ninja combines basic gymnastics, conditioning, flexibility, martial arts, obstacle training and freestyle movement. Kids race timed courses, climb, balance and jump, and are always encouraged to beat their personal best.

Lil' Ninjas · ages 4–8

The entry level. Goals: listening skills, confidence, trying new things, working through frustration and good manners.

Ninja · ages 6 and up

Flips, rolls, jumps and kicks, plus climbing rope, blocks and balance beam. A level system tracks and rewards progress.

Circus Studio

Ages 6 and up · no experience needed

  • Juggling
  • Unicycle
  • Aerial
  • Trapeze
  • Tightrope

Circus Studio blends gymnastics with the circus arts. Kids learn to juggle, ride a unicycle, swing on the trapeze, work on aerial equipment and walk the tightrope, all at safe heights with mats and coach spotting.

  • Hand-eye coordination and focus
  • Balance and body control
  • Upper-body and core strength
  • Creativity and confidence performing

Self-Defense

Ages 7 and up · with Coach Charles

Most of self-defense isn't fighting. It's knowing what's happening around you, and having the confidence to act.

This practical, age-appropriate class helps kids feel safe and self-assured. Students learn to read situations, use a strong voice and posture, set clear boundaries and get away safely if they ever need to. Physical techniques are taught in a calm, structured way, always as a last resort.

  • Awareness of their surroundings
  • A confident voice and body language
  • Recognizing and responding to bullying
  • Simple, safe releases and escapes
  • Staying calm under pressure
  • Respect and self-control
