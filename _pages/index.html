50

50 years coaching gymnastics, from national teams in Belarus, Poland and Italy to New Ipswich.

Head coach Nikolai has led the NIX boys' and girls' programs since 2007. Every class, from a toddler's first forward roll to a team athlete's routine, follows the same step-by-step progressions he has used to develop national-level gymnasts, taught patiently and safely.

Meet our coaches {{arrow}}

Skills that build on each other

Kids learn skills in a deliberate order, so each new one rests on strength and technique they already have. Progress is real, and it's safe.

Boys and girls, every level

NIX coaches both boys' and girls' gymnastics, from beginner classes through state and national competition.

Part of the community

We host open gyms, birthday parties and local events, and our families rally behind the gym with seasonal fundraisers.

Ready to compete?

The NIX Competitive Team trains boys and girls for USA Gymnastics competition, from first meets to state, regional and national championships.

Girls

Vault, uneven bars, balance beam, floor

Boys

Floor, pommel horse, rings, vault, parallel bars, high bar

In 2021, NIX gymnast Aleksandr earned a place on the USA Gymnastics Future Stars National Team for 11-year-olds.
