Competitive team

USA Gymnastics competition for boys and girls, coached by a former national-team coach. Athletes train with purpose, compete across New England and grow into confident, disciplined young people.

How athletes join

There's no tryout day. Coaches get to know each child first, so every athlete starts at the right level.

  1. Start in a class

    Most team athletes begin in recreational or advanced classes, where coaches see their strength, focus and love for the sport.

  2. Coach evaluation

    Already training somewhere else? Request an evaluation. We'll schedule a session to assess skills and readiness.

  3. Join and train

    Athletes receive a team invitation and a training plan with weekly hours, competition level and season goals.

A record of results

In 2021, NIX gymnast Aleksandr earned a place on the Future Stars National Team for 11-year-olds.

Coach Nikolai's athletes have competed at USA Junior Olympic National Championships and USA National Championships. NIX teams have competed at meets such as the Harvest Meet, Gobble Wobble, Judge's Cup, Winter Carnival, Boston Classic, Hip Hop Classic and the New England Championships.
